About

I've spent two decades at the intersection of hospitality and technology — close enough to the kitchen to understand what operators actually need, and technical enough to build it.

That started at Squirrel Systems in 2007, working support and then key accounts for restaurant chains. It ran through Canucks Sports & Entertainment, where I worked across the hospitality technology stack for Rogers Arena and the broader CSE portfolio. It landed at Cactus Club Cafe in 2020, where I've spent six years deploying, integrating, and evolving the platforms that run 41 restaurants.

The domain is POS, KDS, payments, reservations, online ordering, workforce management. I'm also a Certified Scrum Master and Certified Scrum Product Owner — useful when you're sitting at the table where technology meets delivery.

Right now I'm focused on two things: building a technically excellent team, and figuring out what AI actually changes in restaurant operations. Both of those feel like they're just getting interesting.

I live in Vancouver with my wife Missy and our two kids, Jonas and Audrey. The sea is close. That matters.
